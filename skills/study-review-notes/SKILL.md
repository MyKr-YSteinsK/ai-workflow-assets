# Study Review Notes｜已学知识的复盘笔记优化

## Reader and goal
The reader has already studied, read or been taught the material; this does not imply mastery or that every question is resolved. Produce notes for **rapid recall and reconstruction of understanding**, not a substitute textbook or a teaching transcript. Improve the reader's ability to distinguish similar concepts, recover key mechanisms, and find a forgotten detail quickly. Preserve sufficient depth at genuinely difficult links instead of optimizing for brevity alone.

## Establish scope and evidence
- Read the target notes as a whole and inspect directly relevant companion notes, local course material, cited papers, code, or other available sources as needed. Respect the user's requested location, format, organization, and editing permissions.
- Infer **review-only**, **proposed rewrite**, or **actual revision** from the request. A read-only review does not edit, rename or generate files; return prioritized findings with locations, evidence, concrete corrections and important source gaps. “审查并改写/直接优化” authorizes the requested edits; do not force a mode-selection ceremony. A proposed answer is not an applied file change.
- Interpret requests pragmatically: a small cleanup calls for restrained editing; “整体优化/补全/升级/重构笔记” authorizes substantive reorganization, correction, and valuable knowledge expansion within the topic. Do not force the user to choose a mode.
- Existing notes are the starting point, not an infallible authority. Correct demonstrable errors; restore omitted central concepts or conditions using reliable sources or well-established domain knowledge. For nontrivial external additions and contested details, record or cite the source appropriately. If material cannot be substantiated, flag the uncertainty without pretending it has been checked.
- Preserve meaningful personal annotations such as “没理解”, “容易混淆”, “老师强调”, a recalled intuition, open questions, and known misunderstandings. Do not infer or invent the user's mastery level, exam importance, or recurring mistakes merely from topic popularity.

## Inherit the learning project's contract
- Read the actual supplied or accessible project rules relevant to notes: confirmed naming/sequence, stable category/course values, terminology and language policy, Notes Mode, Q&A/reference-answer requirements, and output boundaries. ChatGPT Project attachments are not automatically local; the task may supply these rules directly. Do not require a whole Bootstrap, unavailable templates or an installed skill when the necessary contract is already self-contained.
- Preserve existing valid filenames and links. The learning incubator's `NN-笔记主题_一级目录_课程名.md` applies to its enabled Markdown Notes with confirmed parameters, not to every unrelated note project. Renaming/resequencing needs task authorization and coordinated references; a mismatch in a content-only task is a finding, not permission to migrate filenames. Missing parameters must not be invented.
- Follow the project's terminology policy. For Chinese-first bilingual projects, retain established Chinese/English terms and useful abbreviation expansions at introductions, key explanations and renewed review contexts; avoid first-use-only support or translating every sentence. Explicit alternative language policies take precedence; exact identifiers, APIs and commands remain intact.
- If no project contract exists, follow the user's stated format and useful existing conventions without imposing a global template. If an essential naming/output rule is missing, report or obtain that specific input before the dependent operation; unrelated authorized content review can continue.
- Note review or improved reference answers do not prove mastery, advance a learning stage, change the curriculum, or grant global Checkpoint ownership. The designated Main integrates actual learning evidence under the project's rules; this Skill does not replace the nine-state learning orchestrator or the incubator's one-time candidate audit.

## Restructure for review value
1. **Build fast recall anchors.** Give each major section a compact statement, mechanism cue, recognizable formula/condition/example, or short sequence that brings back prior understanding. Remove transcript-style filler and repetition that adds no review value; preserve unique insights, personal cues and difficult intermediate steps rather than assuming taught material is mastered.
2. **Compare what is confusable.** When multiple related methods, terms, or formulas appear, emphasize discriminative differences: assumptions, inputs/outputs, learned versus fixed quantities, scope, typical application, failure conditions, and how to choose among them. Use a concise comparison table when it is more useful than prose; never fill cells with artificial differences.
3. **Clarify difficulty precisely.** Repair missing links in derivations, algorithms, code/data flow, arguments and causal explanations enough to reconstruct the reasoning: prerequisites, intermediate steps, why each step follows, boundaries and consequences. For consequential complex formulas, preserve assumptions, symbols, units and interpretation; when calculation materially aids recovery or the project requires it, show concrete substitutions, relevant intermediate arithmetic and the interpreted result. Use enough interacting factors to reveal the hard mechanism rather than ending at a trivial one-step case; simpler cues are sufficient where they actually restore understanding. Mark constructed example numbers separately from measured results; do not force numeric examples in unsuitable domains.
4. **Restore the knowledge map.** Use section order, brief cross-links, and occasional relationship diagrams to show prerequisites, related approaches, and conceptual boundaries. Preserve navigable headings and existing links. Avoid mandatory graph maps or a separate note per atomic idea.
5. **Improve substance when warranted.** Repair missing central information, misleading definitions, broken derivations, inconsistent symbols, or gaps that make comparison impossible. Add the minimum sufficient prerequisite bridge and relevant mechanism/application/limitation when omission prevents reconstruction. Distinguish established facts, disputed interpretations and new clarifications with appropriate support; do not expand into an unrelated course or invent a false consensus. Large restructuring is welcome when it clearly improves review; preserve important technical meaning and traceable material.
6. **Maintain natural, efficient language.** Favor direct statements and high information density, avoiding repetitive AI-style prefaces, self-invented “not X but Y” contrasts, ornamental negations and dramatic summaries. Chinese and English follow the same semantic quality principles. Maintain consistent specialist terminology and notation.

## Existing question–answer sections
- If the input notes already have review questions, exercises, self-check prompts, or a Q&A section, improve question quality, correct answers, explain hard distinctions briefly, and add **a small number of high-value questions** when essential concepts or confusion points are under-covered.
- Preserve valuable existing questions, worked reasoning, examples and unresolved issues when reorganizing; do not delete the section merely to fit a cleaner template. Keep meaningful learner-authored answers identifiable when correcting them: separate the reference correction and reason rather than rewriting a mistaken attempt as if the user originally answered correctly.
- Prefer discriminative, causal, mechanism-reconstruction, boundary, and transfer questions over generic definition repetition. Answers should be concise but sufficient for checking one's reasoning; use worked steps for nontrivial calculations.
- Preserve genuinely unresolved questions as unresolved, with a precise gap or next source to check. Do not manufacture user answers, learning performance, or “mastered” status.
- Provide a correct reference answer for every new review question; preserve or repair existing answers according to scope, and explain nontrivial reasoning/calculation. Do not turn unanswered personal questions into invented learner responses.
- Without a question section, do not add a compulsory quiz by default. If the user's request or originating project's enabled Notes contract requires understanding/transfer checks, add a few relevant questions with reference answers even when the input omitted them; this is project-specific compliance, not a universal format.

## Integrity and editing boundaries
- Protect equations, variable meanings, dimensions, code examples, algorithms, numerical results, claims, quotations, links, and genuine limitations. If repairing them, base changes on a reliable explanation and retain the relevant context.
- Distinguish original study material, user observations, and added clarifications when that distinction matters to accuracy or future study. Do not silently merge contradictory explanations into a confidently stated false consensus.
- Preserve the meaning of personal annotations, including through section moves. An added explanation does not justify deleting “没理解” or marking its question resolved without actual evidence; do not invent the speaker or provenance of an annotation. Protect unrelated user changes and update affected headings/links within the authorized scope.
- Follow the requested breadth. Substantial note restructuring and supported corrections to examples inside the authorized notes are permitted; changes to unrelated repositories, course schedules, curricula, or implementation source code are outside scope unless explicitly requested.
- A good finished note is skimmable first and supports focused rereading second. Avoid compulsory templates, excessive headings, mandatory flashcards, artificial diagrams, bulky indexes, and massive auto-generated exercise sets.

## Completion
For review-only, return findings and proposed corrections without file changes. For authorized revision, write to the specified destination and report the actual changes; preserve naming unless changes are separately authorized. Briefly state substantial reorganizations, added core knowledge, important corrections, retained personal/Q&A content and unresolved evidence gaps. Do not claim external facts were checked, a file was installed into ChatGPT, or learning state changed when that did not happen.

Do not produce a separate pedagogy report, mastery score or scoring rubric unless requested. Formal Plan/Handoff follows the actual task's return contract; ordinary note work needs only a concise result. Learning orchestration states, execution status and note-quality findings have separate meanings, not one shared completion label.
